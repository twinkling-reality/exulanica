# ADR-0023: World memory is epistemically typed state, not a privileged representation

## Context

The owned Flatiron district exposed an architectural ambiguity. Its building footprints, heights,
sidewalks, source records, collision, and semantic selection are data-backed. Its facade windows,
cornices, roof furniture, trees, lamps, and visible inhabitants are substantially completed by
browser code. Improving those loops can make a better image while making no better world memory:
the additions cannot necessarily be queried, edited, exported, corrected, trained on, or traced to
an interpretation record.

The obvious correction, “make everything data”, is also wrong. Renderer triangulation, batching,
interpolation frames, mipmaps, and tone mapping have no independent world meaning. Persisting every
vertex would confuse implementation identity with world identity and would make representation
changes look like changes to the world.

The wider research field does not supply one representation that resolves the tension. Generative
video world models provide rich dynamics without durable inspectable state. Persistent generated
3D worlds provide editability without historical evidence semantics. Object scene graphs provide
identity and relations but lose spatial detail. Dense maps and neural fields preserve detail but
are costly and difficult to update semantically. Digital-twin scene descriptions provide
composition but do not provide personal epistemic and consent guarantees.

## Decision

Exulanica's canonical world memory is a composition of **epistemically typed state planes**:
observation, interpretation, spatial, authored, generated, simulation, representation, session,
and governance.

The governing invariant is:

> Everything consequential is addressable; not everything rendered is canonical data.

A feature is consequential when it affects claims, identity, selection, query, accessibility,
measurement, collision, navigation, simulation, persistence, undo, export, model conditioning,
rights, withdrawal, or a promised editable visual property.

Consequential state requires:

- stable subject identity;
- world, branch, and temporal scope;
- epistemic class and authority;
- provenance and source dependencies;
- version or event position;
- declared permitted uses; and
- invalidation/withdrawal behavior.

Derived representations require a contract naming inputs, producer/version, frame, resolution,
preserved and lost properties, admissible uses, quality, digest, and invalidation state. No
representation (mesh, splat, point map, voxel grid, embedding, scene graph, or latent tensor) is
privileged as the whole world.

Procedural output may remain compact. A durable parametric grammar and materialization receipt can
stand behind many disposable render primitives. An emitted feature receives its own stable identity
when it becomes individually selectable, editable, simulated, persistent, accessible, or
queryable.

Models remain sensors, proposers, generators, or predictors behind typed boundaries. They do not
become state authority by producing a plausible output.

## Current capability wording

“Personal World Memory Model” names the complete system architecture and research direction.
In the terminology of machine-learning research, the implementation provides an
evidence-grounded descriptive world-memory substrate with bounded deterministic behaviors.

Exulanica must not claim a general learned predictive world model until action-conditioned,
held-out future prediction satisfies the gates in
[world-memory-model.md](../world-memory-model.md#7-dynamics-and-the-claim-to-prediction).
It must not claim a general generative world model merely because it can record externally
generated artifact receipts.

## Relationship to ADR-0008 and the generated tier

ADR-0008's refusal remains in force for the **recorded reconstruction ladder**, evidence, recorded
geometry, navigation, collision, and unlabeled display.

This decision corrects ADR-0008 Decision 1 only where it says no generated record may enter the
artifact table, scene graph, query path, or World Memory Package under any distinct tier. The
implemented `generated_scene` receipt may be stored and returned as explicitly generated,
non-citable, non-rung state. That metadata admission does not admit generated bytes to rendering,
navigation, collision, evidence, or package export.

Serving, rendering, editing, simulating, or exporting generated geometry remains separately gated.
Any later admission must preserve the separate plane, visible seam, conditioning lineage, rights,
people safeguards, and non-promotion rules. It must state which remaining ADR-0008 checklist items
it satisfies or supersedes.

## Owned-district consequence

The admitted Flatiron records remain source authority for the properties they actually contain.
The current browser completion is classified as a visual prototype, not a complete world-memory
interpretation.

Further district work must:

1. compile road, facade, roof, ground, vegetation, furniture, and activity interpretations into
   versioned engine-neutral records where those features are consequential;
2. distinguish source attributes from inferred structure and authored additions;
3. retain generator/compiler receipts and uncertainty;
4. derive renderer batches, collision proxies, semantic selection, simulation affordances, and
   exports from shared subjects;
5. keep the 24 rendered inhabitants as a view budget over durable population state; and
6. permit raw observation, point/field, semantic, provenance, and rendered views without changing
   world identity.

## Alternatives rejected

### Renderer scene graph as the world

Rejected because engine entities and batches are lifecycle and performance objects. LOD, remount,
or renderer replacement would rewrite identity, and meaningful procedural additions would have no
portable explanation.

### Persist every vertex and generated instance

Rejected because most primitives have no semantic meaning, representation changes would create
false world diffs, and storage volume would rise without improving truth, query, or editability.

### One universal scene schema

Rejected because observations, beliefs, topology, simulation, rendering, and policy have different
authority and change rules. A universal schema either becomes an unbounded property bag or forces
false equivalence.

### One learned latent state as authority

Rejected because latent representations are lossy, model-version dependent, difficult to inspect,
and unsuitable as sole authority for citation, consent, deletion, stable identity, or exact undo.
They may remain useful derived representations when their task benefit is measured.

### Freeze visual work until every object is modeled

Rejected because renderer-only lighting, shading, tessellation, and other non-consequential detail
are legitimate. The materialization threshold decides; “visual” does not automatically mean
“meaningless,” and “procedural” does not automatically mean “canonical.”

## Consequences

Positive:

- representation changes do not rewrite world identity;
- meaningful visual content cannot hide permanently in renderer loops;
- models receive task-appropriate views with explicit loss and use contracts;
- corrections and withdrawals can invalidate every dependent materialization;
- observed, authored, generated, and simulated states can coexist without truth leakage; and
- the research claim has falsifiable gates.

Costs:

- an interpretation compiler and representation-contract vocabulary must be built;
- some existing browser completion must move upstream or be demoted to non-consequential style;
- shared subject identity must bridge semantic, spatial, simulation, and rendering projections;
- multi-representation invalidation and evaluation are more work than one scene export; and
- “world model” must be qualified carefully until prediction is demonstrated.

## Validation

The architecture is accepted by this decision. Its distinct value is not.

The experiments WMM-1 through WMM-10 in
[world-memory-model.md](../world-memory-model.md#10-falsifiable-research-program) determine whether
hybrid memory, representation plurality, provenance, branching, correction, prediction, synthetic
society, and interoperability justify their complexity. A failing comparison narrows or removes
the corresponding layer; it is not explained away by the ambition of the product.
