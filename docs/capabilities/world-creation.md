# World creation

Change the look of your world, add objects, and combine places into your own creations.

Appearance covers colors, textures, and style. Creative composition extends this to authored
objects and arrangements. Alternate versions preserve the source world while allowing changes.
Real and fictional material retain their source and role within the creation.

## Implementation

Reviewed appearance capabilities and preview/apply/rollback contracts exist. General object
editing, combining places into authored versions, and complete alternate-world persistence are
roadmap extensions. Component version histories alone do not implement alternate worlds.

See [appearance contracts](../atlas-world-customization-contract.md),
[appearance persistence](../world-style-backend.md), and the
[world state contract](../product-direction.md#world-state-contract).
