## Reconstruction boundary

The historical reconstruction ladder describes representations of observed source material.
Generated completion cannot become evidence for an unseen surface. Appearance quality does not
establish metric scale, safe navigation or complete coverage. Exact gate behavior belongs to the
linked reconstruction contracts and their evidence.

The original rung table contains assumptions and corrections; it must not be copied into a new
contract as implemented behavior. In particular, posed relief and trained splats are different
representations, and training completion alone does not establish a usable place.

## Research scope

The photograph corpus, model catalog observations, cost estimates, identity experiments and early
MVP constraints in the historical revision apply to their stated inputs and dates. They do not
limit all world creation to photographs or establish provider availability, deployment status,
privacy guarantees or present model quality. Follow the successor owner and its scoped evidence
before implementing or making a public claim.
