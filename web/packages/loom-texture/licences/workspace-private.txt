Exulanica workspace-private texture data

The texture data in this asset was generated by the Exulanica software from a recipe held in one
workspace, for that workspace alone. It is not published and it carries no public licence.

It may be shown to the people who can open that workspace. It is not included in any export,
package, dataset, public page or published library, and every path that publishes refuses it.
It is deleted when the workspace is deleted, and a recipe it was baked from, if withdrawn, stops
it being shown at once.
