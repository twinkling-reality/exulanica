1440x900, one crowd of 128 catalog inhabitants placed by CharacterCrowdEvaluation with 8 of them

the camera frustum in every configuration (the "visible" field), and none was still loading
("pending" 0). Both machine-wide slots were held for the whole run (quiet-slot, then gpu-slot) and the
one-minute load average stayed between 5.62 and 6.87, under the 8 above which a timing run here is
not trustworthy.

Fields: workP50Ms and workP95Ms are the main thread's own time from the app's frameupdate to its
frameend, which is what saturates first; frameP95Ms and framesOver16_7Ms are the presented frame
intervals, which stay at 60 Hz even where the work does not fit. drawCallsP50 and trianglesP50 are
the engine's own counters for the whole scene, preview city included. host is the character host's
resident cost: containers held, encoded bytes, geometry bytes and texture bytes.

The rule, fixed before this run: the budget is the largest measured count whose work p95 stays within
half a 60 Hz frame, 8.3 ms, with presented frames still at 60 Hz. Half is left for the city, the
society simulation, the interface and machines slower than this one. By that rule the budget is 24
full characters (work p95 7.5 ms); 36 is the first count that breaks it, at 9.0 ms. A full character
costs 0.18 ms of frame work at the 95th percentile, a far figure about 0.01 ms.
character-budget.test.ts reads this file and fails if the constant, the rule or the load disagree.
