1. SILHOUETTE COVERAGE AT 5 m AND 30 m (bench.measureCutoutCoverage). A 4 m square of the field, face on, at each
   distance; the footprint is the same square drawn as an opaque unlit mask; a footprint pixel is covered where the
   cutout frame differs from the empty frame by more than 30 in summed RGB. "kept" is the runtime's binding
   (coveragePreservingMips); "plain" is the same material with the device's averaged mip chain, for comparison only.
                     footprint px   kept coverage          plain coverage
   dense   5 m        264,196       0.7071                 0.7078
   dense  30 m          7,396       0.7370 (+4.2 %)        0.7795 (+10.1 %)
   sparse  5 m        264,196       0.3876                 0.3880
   sparse 30 m          7,396       0.3832 (-1.1 %)        0.3657 (-5.7 %)
   Acceptance (30 m within 10 per cent of 5 m): met for both densities with the kept levels. Plain averaging leaves
   the dense field 10.1 per cent thicker and thins the sparse one by 5.7 per cent. Partly covered pixels at leaf edges
   count as covered, which favours coverage at distance slightly for both.

2. THE SHADOW PASS APPLIES THE SAME TEST (bench.measureCutoutShadow). A horizontal 4 m square of the dense field 2.5 m
   above lit paving, the sun straight down, 1,600 ground samples under it seen obliquely from 7 m (not through the
   square). A sample is shadowed when it is darker than open light by more than half of the opaque square's shadow.
   lit mean 227, opaque shadow mean 92; shadowed share: opaque square 1.000, cutout square 0.494 (coverage 0.705).
   The cutout's shadow has holes, so coverage is tested in the shadow pass. Its share is below the coverage because
   the shadow map's filtering and resolution soften leaf edges; it is not a claim that shadow share equals coverage.

cc0.broadleaf-foliage measures 561 per mille coverage.

Silhouette coverage of a 4 m square face on, share of its footprint the leaves cover:

  distance   coverage-preserving chain   plain averaged chain
  5 m        0.5623                      0.5622
  10 m       0.5718                      0.5720

  30 m against 5 m:  kept +2.2 per cent, plain +12.1 per cent.

The published set drifts the other way from the test field, which thinned: this foliage THICKENS on
plain levels, because averaging pulls the many partly covered texels at leaf edges up over the cutoff
rather than under it. Either way the drift is the mip chain's, and the coverage-preserving chain holds
the silhouette to 2.2 per cent over the distance a street is walked, inside the 10 per cent the brief
asks for, where the plain chain is outside it at 12.1.
